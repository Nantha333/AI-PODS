How we measure value

Agreed upfront. Reviewed every quarter.

Value isn’t based on Mako’s opinion. It’s measured against the goals, baselines, and success criteria you agree on from the start.

  1. Establish the baselineBefore development begins, we document current performance using your existing data.
  2. Define measures and targetsTogether, we agree on what success looks like and how each goal will be measured.
  3. Track real-world resultsWe use production data and verifiable reports to track progress against agreed targets.
  4. Review and re-prioritizeEvery quarter, we review results with your business stakeholders, identify what’s working and what isn’t, and adjust the roadmap accordingly.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still deciding? Start with one goal and we’ll map a pod around it.

What is an AI Pod?

A dedicated, cross-functional team built around your AI goals, with defined resources, defined deliverables and one engagement lead.

What do we receive when we start?

A goal sheet with baseline measures, a recommended pod shape (roles and size) and a pod charter covering deliverables, release cadence and production responsibilities, all for your sign-off before building starts.

How are deliverables agreed?

They are written into the pod charter during planning and signed off by your business owners. Changes go through the same review, so priorities stay tied to value.

Who runs the AI in production?

During the engagement, the pod operates what it ships. Monitoring, support hours, escalation and any handover to your team are agreed in the pod charter before go-live.

How is value measured?

Against the baselines and targets agreed at the start, using data from your production systems. Results are reviewed with your business owners every quarter.

How is an AI Pod different from an FDE?

An FDE is one engineer embedded in your business to solve a specific problem. An AI Pod is a full team that runs an ongoing AI roadmap across several use cases.

Can the pod change size?

Yes. As goals change, the pod’s roles and size can be adjusted at a quarterly review, with the change agreed in the charter.

Is our data safe?

Mako is ISO 27001 certified, and every pod works inside your security policies, access controls and data governance rules.
